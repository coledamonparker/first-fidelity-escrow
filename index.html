ABOUT FIRST FIDELITY ESCROW

OVER 20 YEARS OF ESCROW EXCELLENCE

First Fidelity Escrow is an independent escrow company founded in 2014 by Melissa Carson. Melissa has over 20 years of escrow experience and works with many of the top-producing real estate agents and lenders in the nation.

She holds a Bachelor of Science Degree from San Diego State University, is an active member of the California Escrow Association and the North San Diego County Escrow Association, and served on the North San Diego County Escrow Association Board of Directors.

Supported by our dedicated escrow team — including Senior Escrow Officer Nina Latham and Darianne Brewer — First Fidelity Escrow prides itself on exceeding the expectations of real estate agents, lenders, buyers, and sellers on every transaction.

“As the owner, I personally welcome you to bring all of your escrow needs. I assure you that your transaction will be handled with the professionalism it deserves.” — Melissa Carson, Founder & Owner
