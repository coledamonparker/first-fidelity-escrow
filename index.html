About First Fidelity Escrow

Over Two Decades of Trusted Escrow Leadership

First Fidelity Escrow is an independent escrow company founded in 2014 by Melissa Carson. With more than 20 years of escrow experience, Melissa partners with many of the highest-producing real estate agents and mortgage lenders in the nation.

A graduate of San Diego State University (B.S.), Melissa is an active member of the California Escrow Association and the North San Diego County Escrow Association, where she also served on the Board of Directors. Her commitment to continuing education ensures every file strictly adheres to Federal, State, and Local Fiduciary and Escrow Laws.

Alongside Senior Escrow Officer Nina Latham and Darianne Brewer, our team treats every client like family — answering questions promptly, verifying every detail, and ensuring smooth, stress-free closings.

“As the owner, I personally welcome you to bring all of your escrow needs. I assure you that your transaction will be handled with the professionalism it deserves.”

— Melissa Carson, Founder & Owner
