Our 1031 Tax-Deferred Exchanges Process

Every file is managed by our licensed San Diego escrow team in strict accordance with California DFPI regulations.

Qualified Intermediary (QI) Integration

Direct preparation and execution of Exchange Assignment Agreements, Notice of Assignment to all parties, and QI settlement statements.

Direct Deed & Proceeds Protection

Strict adherence to IRS safe-harbor rules: direct deeding to/from the Exchanger while routing net exchange proceeds directly to the QI trust account.

45-Day & 180-Day Timeline Tracking

Proactive milestone monitoring so replacement property acquisitions fund well ahead of statutory IRS deadlines.

Order Preparation Guide

What You Need to Open a 1031 Tax-Deferred Exchanges Escrow

Gather the details below to complete the online intake form. Don’t worry if a few items are still pending — our escrow desk at orders@ffescrow.com can open your file and issue an Escrow Number right away.

Proceed to Open Order Form
  • Subject Property Address and Indication of Exchange Phase (Relinquished Downleg Sale vs. Replacement Upleg Purchase)
  • Exchanger (Taxpayer) Full Legal / Entity Name and Exact Tax-Return Vesting (must match across legs)
  • Contract Price, Earnest Money Deposit, and Target Close of Escrow Date
  • Qualified Intermediary (QI) Company Name, Exchange Coordinator Name, Phone, Email, and Exchange #
  • Statutory 45-Day Identification Deadline and 180-Day Exchange Completion Deadline (for Replacement Purchases)
  • Counterparty (Buyer or Seller) Names and Contact Details
  • Real Estate Agents/Brokers and Replacement Property Mortgage Lender Contact Information
